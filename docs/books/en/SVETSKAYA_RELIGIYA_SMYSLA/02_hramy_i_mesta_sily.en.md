# Temples and Places of Power

## Why such Spaces are Needed

People need places where they can not only consume content, but also think together, learn, and do useful things.

It is not about religious institutions, but about **spaces of social maturation**.

## Types of Spaces

- **Workshops of Action**  
  Places where people repair, design, teach, and share skills together.

- **Halls for Reviewing Mistakes**  
  A format for open analysis of failures without bullying, with the aim of improving practices.

- **Observatories and Science Clubs**  
  Places where respect for knowledge and a large-scale view of the world are developed.

- **Centers for Public Dialogue**  
  Platforms for negotiations, local solutions, and joint initiatives.

## Principle of Operation

A good place of power:

- does not produce a cult of the leader;
- does not require blind loyalty;
- measures value through contribution and quality of interaction.

## Criterion of Usefulness

If after participation people become calmer, more accurate in thinking, and more active in creative actions, the space fulfills its function.
