# Rituals without Coercion

## What is Understood here by Ritual

These are regular actions that help to maintain attention, discipline, and respect for each other.  
They should not be a tool of pressure.

## Why they are Needed

Without repeatable practices, values quickly fall apart into beautiful words.  
With practices, values turn into stable behavior.

## Examples of Neutral Practices

- short morning focus: "what I will improve today";
- weekly review of team mistakes without searching for a "culprit";
- socially useful action according to the schedule (repair, help, training);
- personal practice of fact-checking before spreading information.

## Boundary between Practice and Violence

A practice becomes harmful when:

- there is no right to refuse or pause;
- humiliation for "insufficient involvement" is used;
- form becomes more important than meaning and result.

## Principle of Voluntariness

Participation must be conscious.  
A strong practice invites, rather than breaks.
