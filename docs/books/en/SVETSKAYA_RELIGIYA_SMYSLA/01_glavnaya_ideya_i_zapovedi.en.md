# Main Idea and Commandments

## Central Thought

A person is not just a consumer of services and goods.  
He is a participant in common development, on which the quality of life of other people and the future of society depend.

## Basic Framework

This book offers a practical ethics in which:

- meaning is born in action, not in declarations;
- human dignity is more important than profit at any cost;
- knowledge and constructive action are more important than information noise.

## Five Supporting Principles

1. **Truth and Verifiability**  
   Speak and act so that words can be checked.

2. **Constructive Action instead of Exploitation**
   Evaluate decisions by real benefit for people, rather than by short-term profit.

3. **Sufficiency and Responsibility for Resources**  
   Consume reasonably, without shifting the environmental and social debt to others.

4. **Growth through Learning and Revision**  
   Changing a position based on new data is a sign of maturity.

5. **Solidarity and Respect for Dignity**  
   One cannot build "one's success" on the humiliation or dehumanization of others.

## Short Conclusion

This framework does not require a single ideology.  
It requires honesty of thinking, responsibility for consequences, and respect for the person.
