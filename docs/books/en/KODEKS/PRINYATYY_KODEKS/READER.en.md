# Accepted Code: Reader's Guide

A short guide to the main ideas and tools of the Code.

## How to read

1. Read 3 basic texts (15-20 minutes).
2. Choose 1-2 checklists for your role (editor, manager, teacher, community member).
3. Apply to one real decision.

## Step 3. Mini-practice

Take one current situation (work project, publication, controversial decision) and answer:

- where is the real benefit for people here;
- where is the risk of harm;
- what data confirms the decision;
- what needs to be changed before launch.

If after checking you see that the decision has become clearer and more honest, the Code is working.
