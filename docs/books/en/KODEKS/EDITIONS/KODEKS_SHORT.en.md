# Codex of Constructive Society (Short Edition)

## Why this document exists

The Codex is a practical framework to:

- distinguish constructive action from destructive action;
- rely on truth and verifiable facts;
- make decisions that preserve human dignity;
- align personal responsibility, common good, and a long-term horizon.

It is not an ideology and not a slogan set. It is a working standard for behavior and decisions.

## 15 Core Principles

1. **Truth is more important than convenient lies.**
2. **Human dignity is inviolable.**
3. **Freedom is not the right to harm others.**
4. **Constructive action is more important than gain at any cost.**
5. **Technology is justified only when it improves quality of life.**
6. **Bioprogress is acceptable only where humans are not treated as commodities.**
7. **Capital is a tool, not the ultimate goal.**
8. **Knowledge must remain verifiable and revisable.**
9. **Journalists, media creators, and educators are responsible for the quality of public thinking.**
10. **Care for close relationships is part of social construction.**
11. **Capacity for agreement is stronger than escalation.**
12. **National identity strengthens constructive action, not hostility.**
13. **Earth and space form one development contour.**
14. **Human rights and development are compatible, not opposed.**
15. **Faith and secular ethics can coexist in shared public space.**

## How to apply the Codex

Before any major action, ask four questions:

1. Who is genuinely helped by this?
2. What harm is possible, and how is it reduced?
3. Which sources support this decision?
4. What happens in 5–10 years if this practice scales?

If answers are unclear, the decision should be revised.
