# Codex: Working Division

This is the only official Codex of the project.

This folder fixes the fundamental division:

- `PRINYATYY_KODEKS/` — the active, stable, applicable part.
- `KONTEKST_EPOKHI/` — analytics, external conditions, scenarios, and discussion materials.
- `EDITIONS/` — complete reader editions (short and full versions).

## Why this is Important

The Codex must be executable and understandable "here and now."  
The planet's context is changing rapidly, so it is better to keep it separate from the normative core.
