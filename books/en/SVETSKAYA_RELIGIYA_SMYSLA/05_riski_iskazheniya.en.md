# Risks of Distortion

## Why this Section

Any strong idea can be distorted.  
To prevent the Code from turning into an ideology of pressure, the risks must be named in advance.

## Main Risks

1. **Cult of the Only Correct Version**  
   The space for questions, criticism, and revision disappears.

2. **New Moral Elitism**  
   People begin to divide into "pure" and "incorrect" instead of help and development.

3. **Digital Control without Boundaries**  
   Metrics and ratings replace living ethics and human dignity.

4. **Replacing Meaning with Procedure**  
   The form is observed, but there is no real benefit and honesty.

## Protection Mechanisms

- the right to reasoned disagreement;
- independent review of controversial decisions;
- transparent criteria and public adjustments;
- mandatory check for compatibility with human rights.

## Quick Test "Have we gone into Distortion?"

- can current rules be safely criticized;
- is there a way to correct errors without humiliation;
- does the system help people become more mature, rather than more dependent.

If there is no confident "yes" to these questions, the framework needs to be revised.
