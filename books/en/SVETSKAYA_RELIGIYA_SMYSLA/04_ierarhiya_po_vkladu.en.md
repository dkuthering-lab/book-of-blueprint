# Hierarchy by Contribution

## Why this is Important

If society respects only money and consumption status, it encourages cynicism.  
The Code offers another logic: recognition by the quality of contribution to common development.

## What is Considered a Contribution

- work that really improves people's lives;
- honest transmission of knowledge and upbringing of the next generation;
- creation of solutions that reduce chaos and injustice;
- actions that strengthen trust and negotiability.

## What is NOT Considered a Contribution

- profit built on manipulation and exploitation;
- conspicuous consumption as "proof of value";
- status without responsibility for consequences.

## How to Avoid a New Elitism

1. Evaluation criteria must be public.
2. The possibility of growth must be open to any person.
3. An error does not exclude from the community if the person recognizes it and corrects it.

## Short Conclusion

A hierarchy by contribution is needed not to divide people into "castes," but for a fair distribution of respect and responsibility.
