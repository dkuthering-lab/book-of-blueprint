# Human Code

Source: central block of the dialogue about the book structure and practical pillars.

## Book Structure

- `books/en/CODEX_CHELOVEKA/01_tom_osnovanie.en.md` — Science as revelation (cosmogenesis, biogenesis, noogenesis).
- `books/en/CODEX_CHELOVEKA/02_tom_put.en.md` — four pillars: order, return of resource, sufficiency, expansion of the horizon.
- `books/en/CODEX_CHELOVEKA/03_tom_zhitiya.en.md` — biographical case studies: models of choice, lessons, and practices of memory.
- `books/en/CODEX_CHELOVEKA/04_ritualy_i_povsednevnye_praktiki.en.md` — morning rule, entry practices, crisis readings.
- `books/en/CODEX_CHELOVEKA/05_sovmestimost_s_veryashchimi_traditsiyami.en.md` — how the Code coexists with Orthodoxy without conflict.
- `books/en/CODEX_CHELOVEKA/06_media_kreatory_kak_provodniki_smysla.en.md` — media and psychology as part of the service to knowledge.
- `books/en/CODEX_CHELOVEKA/07_pedagogi_i_vospitateli_seyateli.en.md` — the role of educators and teachers as co-authors of the Code.
- `books/en/CODEX_CHELOVEKA/08_blizkie_otnosheniya_i_osoznannyy_vybor.en.md` — closeness as constructive action, mistakes as a signal for work or honest completion.
- `books/en/CODEX_CHELOVEKA/09_zhurnalistika_kak_sluzhenie_istine_i_obshchestvu.en.md` — journalism as an important labor of public visibility of truth, successes, and problems.
- `books/en/CODEX_CHELOVEKA/10_krasnye_flagi_manipulyativnogo_materiala.en.md` — checklist of 5 signs of manipulation in media.
- `books/en/CODEX_CHELOVEKA/11_politika_oborona_i_etika_dogovora.en.md` — negotiability, creative patriotism, and responsibility to humanity.
- `books/en/CODEX_CHELOVEKA/12_pyat_printsipov_peregovorov_v_logike_kodeksa.en.md` — applied principles of negotiations to reduce escalation and preserve the dignity of the parties.
- `books/en/CODEX_CHELOVEKA/13_pyat_priznakov_chto_material_ne_sluzhit_istine.en.md` — checklist for authors and editors about professional self-check.
- `books/en/CODEX_CHELOVEKA/14_tsitirovanie_istochniki_i_obnovlenie_znaniya.en.md` — culture of links, verifiability, and acceptance of knowledge update as development.
